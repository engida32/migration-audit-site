Questions

The short answers. If yours is not here, email me and I will answer it before you commit to anything.

What counts as a finding?

A specific place in your repository where the migration will break or cost more than it should. Each finding cites a file and line, a severity, and an effort estimate.

Which stacks do you audit?

Python and TypeScript, including Django, Flask, FastAPI, React, Next.js, and Node services. I do not take PHP, Ruby, or .NET work.

Do you change my code?

No. Your original checkout is never modified. I work on a copy in a sandbox and include two verified sample diffs as separate files. Applying them is your call.

What if you find nothing material?

You get the evidence and a short note saying so. I do not invent findings to fill a report.

How is my code handled?

Your original checkout is never modified. Analysis runs on a copy in a network-off Docker sandbox. Job data is deleted 30 days after delivery unless you ask me to keep it.
